## Honesty rules applied in the cut
- Every interface scene shows the label **"Prototype interface · demonstration data"**, and the end card says **"Concept film · interfaces shown with demonstration data"**.
- Municipal integration is narrated as *"Designed to connect with…"*. No municipality is named in the picture as a partner, and the map badge reads "Simulated GIS · sample area".
- The Building ID is presented as a digital reference identity. Nothing claims legal title, certification or government endorsement.
- All names, buildings, IDs and figures are fictional demo data from the BENA prototype.

## Sound
- **Score:** original instrumental, generated for this film. Cinematic ambient with felt piano and soft pulse; builds from 0:46 and peaks around 1:14.
- **Ducking:** the score ducks under the narration through a sidechain, and dips a further 6 dB during the sunrise.
- **Sound design:** dusk city ambience for the opening, soft whooshes on the major transitions, glassy interface taps on device moments, and low "bloom" reveals on both logos.
- **Master:** −14 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBTP.
